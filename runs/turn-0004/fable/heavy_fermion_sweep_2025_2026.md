# Heavy-fermion sweep, 2025–2026 (turn 4, Fable)

Question: any genuinely new ambient-pressure heavy-fermion / Kondo-lattice superconductor with Tc > ~3 K reported in 2025–2026?

Answer: **No.** (A sub-agent sweep ended without a report; the entries below are from my own web searches, with abstract/paper excerpts read directly; identifiers below were all returned by the search tool and checked against the excerpt.)

| Compound | Tc | Ambient? | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CeSiI (vdW heavy-fermion metal) | 0.24 K max | No, dome at 6–9 GPa | arXiv:2601.18476; Nature Physics (July 2026, per CAS press release) | "first vdW heavy-fermion superconductor"; AF TN=7.5 K at ambient, no ambient SC |

Highest ambient-pressure Tc among f-electron heavy-fermion superconductors remains PuCoGa5 (18.5 K; Sarrao et al., Nature 420, 297 (2002)), as tabulated in runs/turn-0002/outputs/hf_table.csv. Nothing found for 2025–2026 moves that.

Conclusion for the compass direction: the family remains a low-priority route to high Tc. Turn 3's criticism of the turn-2 "28 K ceiling" (it is an empirical bound extracted from a sample, not a theorem) is accepted; but two more years of literature do not change the empirical picture: new heavy-fermion superconductors keep appearing at sub-K to ~4 K, usually under pressure, and are destroyed by a few percent non-magnetic disorder. I recommend the lab stop spending turns here unless a concrete ≥10 K ambient-pressure candidate appears.
