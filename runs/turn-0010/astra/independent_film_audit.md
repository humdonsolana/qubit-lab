## Verified quantities and distinctions

### September interlayer-hybridization study

**Cap:** approximately **1 nm amorphous PrBa2Cu3O7 (PBCO)** on LaPr2Ni2O7, deposited **ex situ at room temperature**. [P] Methods specify growth in **10% O3 / 90% O2**, **0.41 mbar** total pressure, **780 °C**, 4 Hz, 1.8 J/cm²; cooling **50–100 °C/min** under the same atmosphere. Thicker films receive **400 °C, 10–20 min** in-situ annealing. Cap deposition uses **1.0 J/cm²** under the same atmospheric conditions. These are preprint-method values, not independently verified final-main-text values.

Final [S], Note 2/Fig S2 verifies the nominal cap thickness through an XRR-calibrated deposition rate of approximately **80 pulses/nm**, using a **13.5 nm PBCO reference film**, and cross-sectional STEM. The 13.5 nm reference is **not** the cap on the superconducting spectroscopy samples. Composition is the nominal target/material designation; direct oxygen stoichiometry of the amorphous cap is not quantified here.

**Transport:** [P], Fig1/text: SC1 **7 nm**, SC2 **8 nm**, metallic film **8 nm**, original insulating film **14 nm**. Both superconducting films have reported **onset approximately 30 K**; SC1 has reported **zero-resistance temperature approximately 10 K**. Do not label this a 30 K zero-resistance result. No precise SC2 zero temperature is claimed in this audit. Spectroscopy raises overall resistance somewhat, but the authors report unchanged onset/zero temperatures. That is evidence of survival, not perfect absence of degradation.

Independent [D] check illustrates the threshold issue: SC1 measured sheet resistance is **0.190055 Ω/sq at 10.1493 K**, **0.006580 Ω/sq at 6.5548 K**, and **−0.003185 Ω/sq at 3.5527 K**. SC2 is **7.517295 Ω/sq at 9.18324 K**, **0.070870 Ω/sq at 3.69185 K**, and fluctuates around a few milliohms near 1.90 K. The low-temperature sign/noise behavior supports an operational near-zero state; it does **not** provide a mathematically exact zero or the measurement-floor criterion underlying the nominal 10 K label. Report that label as the authors' approximation, not a new independently determined Tc.

**Controls:** [S], Note 2/Fig S3: superconductivity verified **before cap deposition**; before/after resistance and critical-current comparison uses an **11.8 nm** film, Hall comparison a **3.5 nm** film. Some controls are reused from [C], hence not an independent replication. PBCO-on-SLAO alone is highly insulating. Cap/substrate O-K XAS lacks the nickelate pre-edge below **529 eV**; a feature near **531.8 eV** has cap/substrate contribution. These controls weaken parallel-cap conduction and cap-generated pre-edge explanations but do not rule out all interface electrochemical effects.

**Oxygen/strain control:** [S], Note 3/Fig S4 tracks a roughly **9 nm** film: initially oxygen-rich metallic, enhanced superconducting transition after approximately **2 months** in air, insulating after **14 months**. Hall coefficient at **50 K** moves from negative toward positive. This is a qualitative oxygen-depletion trajectory, not a measured absolute δ. Note 4 adds initially superconducting **8 nm Insulator2**, made insulating by ambient exposure, and fresh roughly **7 nm** superconducting/metallic comparison films; reciprocal-space maps show coherent substrate-matched in-plane strain. Improved strain matching is valuable, but this is not a fully continuous, same-sample spectroscopic oxygen sweep.

### Half-dome study: different composition and uncapped protocol

**Important naming trap:** [H] uses **La2PrNi2O7+δ** for LPNO and **La2SmNi2O7+δ** for LSNO; [N/P/C] use **LaPr2Ni2O7**. These are not identical rare-earth ratios despite overlapping abbreviations.

[H] deliberately uses **no surface cap**, to enable oxygen tuning. Sequential vacuum annealing in the same 4He cryostat at approximately **360 K** reduces oxygen after ozone loading. PLD growth: **660 °C**, **150 mTorr O2**, 5 Hz, 0.56 J/cm². MBE: substrate pre-anneal **750 °C, 10 min, 5×10^-6 Torr ozone**; growth **680 °C, 1.8×10^-6 Torr ozone**. Oxygen-rich metallic → superconducting → vacancy-rich insulating transport evolves within the same sample; supplement Fig S4 repeats oxygenation/reduction in LPNO, with some crystallinity degradation in the second cycle.

Their operational 'optimal' freshly ozone-annealed films satisfy **Tc,onset >40 K** and **Tc,90%−Tc,10% <10 K**. Fig S2 extracts onset via a linear fit near the onset in **d²ρ/dT²**, with its zero intercept defining onset; 90%, 50% and 10% criteria refer to normal resistance near onset. The 'optimized' annealing step is selected by **highest Tc,10%**, used as a phase-coherence proxy, **not necessarily a zero-resistance criterion**. On the oxygen-deficient side onset stays nearly intact while midpoint collapses; this is the central half-dome observation, not evidence that homogeneous zero-resistance superconductivity survives throughout that side.

Absolute oxygen content is explicitly not measured at the required accuracy. Annealing time and normalized **σ(200 K)** are proxies; normalizing to a Hall minimum does not calibrate δ or remove disorder effects. Calcium series **x=0, 0.165, 0.382** in La2−xPrCaxNi2O7+δ supports similar trends within that study. Their inferred **δ>0.19** for metallic LSNO is model-dependent, not a directly verified oxygen number. Site assignments, granularity and quantum-percolation interpretation remain interpretations of transport/spectroscopy rather than direct spatial imaging of superconducting puddles.

### Relevant oxygen-retention films

[C] primary abstract: nominal nickelate thicknesses **3.5–23.5 nm**, maximum **onset 44 K**, superconductivity confined to approximately **10 nm** from the interface. Thicker films develop a protective **(La,Pr)4Ni3O10 surface layer**; nominal whole-film thickness must not be mistaken for a homogeneous bilayer superconducting thickness. Amorphous oxide capping reportedly gives **10-fold stability enhancement** for roughly **3–4 nm** films and extends thicker-film stability from **30 to >100 days**. These are abstract-level reported durability results, **not independently audited survival thresholds or zero-Tc time series**.

[S] identifies amorphous **SrTiO3** and **LaNiO3** as comparator caps, with PBCO the best tested ambient stabilizer. Comparator thicknesses and their quantitative lifetimes were not recoverable from accessible primary full text; do not assign the 1 nm PBCO thickness to every comparator. Oxygen-vacancy formation energy / low c-axis diffusivity is a proposed retention explanation, not a measured diffusion coefficient for the amorphous cap.

## Falsifiable experiment — no Tc forecast

Use composition-verified, equal-thickness **7–8 nm** LaPr2Ni2O7 films from matched growths on SLAO; pre-register measured oxygen/strain and transport endpoints. Compare uncapped versus **1 nm amorphous PBCO**, with insulating PBCO-only/substrate and matched-thickness SrTiO3/LaNiO3 controls. Prepare both near-optimal and initially oxygen-rich metallic states **before capping**. A retention cap should preserve the starting state, not necessarily improve an overoxygenated film.

Track fixed air and beam/vacuum exposure doses, four-probe R(T), defined instrument-noise-floor zero resistance, critical current and susceptibility/superfluid response, plus polarized O-K/Ni-L XAS and reciprocal-space maps. Use calibrated oxygen quantification and/or isotope-exchange depth profiling on sister specimens; Hall and σ alone cannot disentangle oxygen content from scattering. Keep handling/measurement delays and beam doses equal and report spatial oxygen uniformity.

**Reject the retention-only explanation** if protected transport changes occur without a corresponding reproducible oxygen-retention difference, or cap-only/interface controls account for the conduction. **Challenge necessity of the proposed interlayer signature** if robust zero-resistance plus superfluid response persists while the specified out-of-plane hybridization signature is absent at matched strain, thickness and composition. Hybridization without superconductivity falsifies a sufficiency claim, not necessarily a necessity claim. This experiment tests measurable survival and mechanism, without predicting room-temperature Tc.
