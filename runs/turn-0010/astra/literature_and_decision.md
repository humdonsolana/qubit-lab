## Frontier sources and limits

1. Zhang et al., Nature Materials, 23 September 2026, DOI **10.1038/s41563-026-02750-z**, *Interlayer hybridization promotes superconductivity in bilayer nickelates*. Primary publisher metadata, final SI and peer-review file, author preprint **2604.14701v2**, and published data **10.6084/m9.figshare.33127652** inspected. Composition **LaPr2Ni2O7**, 7–8 nm superconducting layers, ~1 nm amorphous PBCO cap. Do not interchange with La2PrNi2O7. Final main text paywalled; explicit ~30 K onset and SC1 ~10 K zero are author-preprint values, not independent criteria. Final SI cap controls include verification of superconductivity before deposition and insulating cap-only control. Causality was challenged in peer review; title softened from enables to promotes. This is neither a retraction nor proof of a unique mechanism.
2. Liu et al., **arXiv:2603.12196v1**, 12 March 2026, *A superconducting half-dome in bilayer nickelates*. Uncapped La2PrNi2O7 and La2SmNi2O7, same-film oxygen tuning. Oxygen excess suppresses onset toward metal; oxygen loss can preserve onset while coherence-like lower thresholds collapse. Annealing time/conductivity is not calibrated oxygen delta. These are preprint observations, not independent proof of nanoscale percolation.
3. Han et al., **arXiv:2610.01757v1**, 1 October 2026, *Selective suppression of electronic orders via interlayer coupling in superconducting bilayer nickelate thin films*. Read primary HTML/full text and abstract. REXS sees SDW in superconducting films; substitution/oxygen lowers T_SDW from ~150 to ~70 K. Charge-like anisotropy vanishes while SDW remains. Thus a rule requiring complete elimination of static SDW is not supported. Spatial beam resolution ~450 by450 micrometers cannot prove microscopic homogeneous coexistence. Film spectroscopy done within three days; same-film aging R(T) comparison is not a long-term stability guarantee. Preprint, short follow-up window.
4. Liu et al., Nano Letters, 12 September 2026, DOI **10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c03024**, *Superconducting Weak Links at Substrate Steps in Bilayer Nickelate Films*. Main text inaccessible (403), so use Crossref primary abstract and actual publisher SI, Figshare **33669009**, file **68487330**, nl6c03024_si_001.pdf. Abstract: angular two-step transition tracks substrate step direction; transport depends on terrace width. SI S1/S2 shows strain relaxation and extra (La,Pr)2NiO4 accommodation layer near steps. S3/S4 simulate pristine/weak-link stripe regions, not our new simulation. S6 gives a representative as-received terrace width **290 nm**, not an optimum. Important distinct connectivity mechanism; do not attribute every two-step transition to oxygen granularity alone.
5. Shi et al., Advanced Materials DOI **10.1002/adma.202510394**, first online **15 October 2025**, 2026 journal volume. ## Concrete next experiment, intentionally unranked

Target **7–8nm LaPr2Ni2O7 / SrLaAlO4(001), ~1nm amorphous PrBa2Cu3O7 cap**, composition verified. This is a replication/control target, **Tc forecast unestablished**, not a new composition. It does not meet the133K candidate bar. Same prescription cannot be silently transferred to La2PrNi2O7.

Extend the published cap idea with a factorial connectivity/retention test: matched cap/no-cap and controlled terrace-width cohorts (measure actual distributions by AFM, do not assign an unsupported optimal width), common thickness/strain, two initial oxygen states (near-optimal and overoxygenated). On matched films pattern transport parallel/perpendicular to measured step direction; include cap-only insulating and sister-film structural controls. Measure days0,8,20,100 with identical storage/measurement exposure, pre-registered instrument-floor zero, critical current and mutual-inductance/superfluid response. Retain raw low-T offsets; do not subtract a fitted residual just to manufacture zero. Use oxygen-sensitive spectroscopy plus an independently calibrated oxygen or isotope-depth measure; Hall/conductivity alone is not oxygen quantification.

Predictions are falsifiable but non-numerical: cap should reduce oxygen drift, while step control should reduce orientation-dependent separation of upper/lower transitions. Reject retention-only interpretation if transport protection lacks oxygen-retention difference; reject step-only explanation if angular differences do not track verified step geometry at matched oxygen. A cap can lock in an overoxygenated bad state. Improving coherence toward existing onset is not evidence onset itself can exceed133K. To upgrade: demonstrate reproducible higher pairing/onset scale, whole-film phase coherence and retention, rather than extrapolate c-axis or rung exchange.
