## Calculation performed

Run `python pairbreaking_screen.py` (numpy, scipy; matplotlib optional). `calculation.log` contains the actual run output and versions. All data generated by the script are conditional model output, except the explicitly source-derived slopes above.

For hypothetical clean transition T0 and effective pair-breaking energy Gamma:

```
ln(T0/Tc) = digamma(1/2 + Gamma/(2*pi*kB*Tc)) - digamma(1/2)
kB = 0.08617333262145178 meV/K
Gamma_critical = pi*exp(-EulerGamma)*kB*T0/2
```

This is the homogeneous Abrikosov–Gor'kov sensitivity benchmark [7], not a fit to CeCoIn5. Gamma is defined by this equation; **no conversion to transport lifetime, resistivity or dopant fraction is made**. Strong coupling, anisotropic scattering, multiband physics, magnetic droplets, Kondo coherence and spatial inhomogeneity are not explicitly modeled. In particular, reference [2] warns against a literal transport-based AG interpretation here.

The main scenario idealizes the measured 2.3 K reference as clean. Hypothetical gains are input assumptions, not predictions:

| Assumed clean gain | Assumed T0 (K) | Gamma erasing the gain (micro-eV) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5% | 2.415 | 12.52 |
| 10% | 2.530 | 24.87 |
| 20% | 2.760 | 49.07 |
| 50% | 3.450 | 118.60 |

The additional Gamma budget for a 10% intrinsic gain changes from 24.87 to 32.02 micro-eV when an *assumed* pre-existing Gamma is varied from 0 to 0.1 meV while keeping observed Tc at 2.3 K. Thus observed Tc alone cannot identify the intrinsic clean scale.

An independent local-linear calculation uses the source's isolated suppression slopes: a hypothetical 0.23 K gain is canceled by 0.046 Cd, 0.0205 Pt, or 0.0173 Sn atoms per formula unit. Converted to the relevant host-site percentages these are 0.92% of all In sites, 2.05% of Co sites and 0.346% of all In sites. These averages must not be confused with occupancy of the preferentially substituted In(1) sublattice. These are arithmetic sensitivity estimates, NOT proposed compositions or ambient-pressure phase-diagram forecasts. The extraction relies on pressure/codoping and the paper's assumptions; the numbers have no independently estimated confidence intervals. They cannot calibrate Gamma without additional information.

## Falsifiable next step / handoff

Perform zero-field reversible strain on the same high-purity CeCoIn5 crystal. Compare resistance midpoint and width with ac susceptibility and preferably heat capacity; monitor strain, hysteresis and irreversible resistivity changes. A reproducible bulk-transition increase versus the relaxed crystal is the minimum success criterion. Resistive onset alone, increased Hc2 alone, or cracking-driven hysteresis fails this criterion. Externally maintained stress must be labeled as such, and cannot be submitted as an unloaded ambient-pressure result.

Before investing in heterostructure calculations, extract actual zero-field Tc-versus-pressure/thickness data from [3] and compare with equal-thickness nonmagnetic-spacer controls and measured strain. A material-specific forecast needs a validated pairing model or direct measurements; ordinary structure relaxation cannot supply Tc for this mechanism.
