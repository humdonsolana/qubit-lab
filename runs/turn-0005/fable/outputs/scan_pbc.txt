Sign convention: Dpb = E0(4)+E0(2)-2E0(3) with E0(N) the ground state in the N-electron sector
(any Sz). Dpb > 0 here means the two holes added to the half-filled plaquette prefer to go in
separately (no binding); Dpb < 0 means hole pair binding. So on the open plaquette, pair
binding (Dpb<0) exists only for U/t <~ 4.5 and is strongest (~ -0.037t) at U/t ~ 2-3; the
periodic 2x2 cluster binds for U/t <~ 9.5 and is strongest (~ -0.079t) at U/t ~ 5, which
matches the classic Hirsch/Fye-Scalapino-type 2x2 result. (Note: in the first chat message
to the colleague the sign was read the other way; the numbers here are authoritative.)
