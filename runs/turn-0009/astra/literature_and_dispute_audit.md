## 1. LaSc2H24 is disputed high-pressure evidence, not an ambient target
- Song et al., arXiv:2510.01273v2, 6 October 2025 (first submission 29 September 2025): claimed onset 271–298 K at 195–266 GPa, zero resistance/field suppression and 13 runs. Primary abstract checked directly. Treat authors' phase identification and reproducibility claims as claims, not independent confirmation.
- Semenok et al., arXiv:2605.29985v2, 20 August 2026 (first submission 28 May 2026): seven unsuccessful syntheses from La–Sc alloy at 250–280 GPa, no 245–300 K superconductivity; precursor differences remain a possible explanation. Thus neither independent replication nor definitive impossibility proof.
- Crucially, the same follow-up obtains >300 K at 300 GPa with tetrahedron integration. Full PDF Fig.3/text reports harmonic lambda=4.77 and Allen–Dynes Tc up to307 K at mu*=0.1. Their Gaussian-broadening treatment instead varies Tc by50–60K. Their stability parameter xi~0.54 is near an empirical boundary, NOT a universal no-go threshold. It would be misleading to cite this paper as purely a theoretical refutation.
- Decision: no numerical ambient Tc and no candidate. This is not moderate pressure, and no unloaded-phase retention evidence was identified.

## 2. C0001 Mg2IrH6: retained challenge, corrected intrinsic-Tc coverage
**Prior turns omitted an important independent prediction.** Sanna et al., npj Computational Materials10,44 (28 February2024), DOI10.1038/s41524-024-01214-9, Table1, gives for Mg2IrH6 lambda2.1, omega_log50meV; Tc77.0K isotropic Eliashberg,65.9K isotropic SCDFT and71K anisotropic SCDFT. The calculations treat Coulomb interactions and energy-dependent DOS. The discussion gives bare mu=0.58 and argues mu* well above0.25; bare mu must NOT be inserted as mu*. Their abstract's broad45–80K range is not a singleIr estimate.

Hansen et al., arXiv:2406.09538 / DOI10.1103/PhysRevB.110.214513, originally submitted13June2024, is the source of the dense-grid90–120K estimate used by C0001. The chosen105K was the midpoint of that method, not an experimental measurement or a consensus. Adding Sanna requires explicit between-method uncertainty; do not silently average incompatible methods or claim one is proved correct. The much older160–175K optimistic calculation does not establish a credible current route above133K.

`coulomb_sensitivity.py` re-evaluates an Allen–Dynes FORMULA for Sanna's rounded lambda/omega_log, not Sanna's ab-initio DOS-dependent solver. Unknown spectral second moment is varied over all r>=1 and its shape factor analytically maximized. At mu*=0.16 formula range83.79–90.21K; at0.25 it is63.50–66.55K. This demonstrates sensitivity, NOT a rigorous bound on actual Tc. No raw Eliashberg function was reconstructed and no new DFPT/SCDFT run was performed.

Sinha et al., arXiv:2602.23675 (27February2026), primary abstract and PDF conclusion: cubic H7 forms near40GPa and remains insulating; below~20GPa on room-temperature unloading it returns toH5. No superconducting H6 intermediate observed. `phase_access_review.py` rereads Turn4 original PBEsol numerical energies and exactly recovers H6 above H5+H7 by15.88/60.66meV perH6atom, depending on H7 polymorph. Hydrogen chemical potential cancels in2H6→H5+H7. No thermodynamic mu window in that restricted static dataset; no new DFT and not a complete finite-T phase diagram.

**Verdict:** challenge strengthened: unresolved intrinsic theory spread plus failed phase access; retention by itself would still leave current credible numbers below133K. To revive: phase-resolved ambientH6 occupancy and local H probes, metallicity, zero resistance+bulk diamagnetism/thermodynamic anomaly, and aging/field controls. If claiming above133K, demand measured or converged material-specific evidence with Coulomb/DOS treatment, not extrapolation from the early optimistic Tc.

## 3. New related experimental milestone, not ambient calibration
Wu et al., arXiv:2608.15745v3 (9September2026), JACS148,36854, DOI10.1021/jacs.6c08889: Mg2RhH6 reported24K at30GPa and29K at53GPa, zero resistance and field suppression. Primary abstract checked. This is promising evidence of an accessible superconducting member of the octahedral complex-hydride family, not a >133K ambient route. Comparing it to Sanna's ambient45–52K Rh predictions is NOT a controlled factor-of-two theory error: pressures differ. No independent replication established by this audit. Do not transfer a numerical correction factor to Ir.

## 4. 2026 MgAlFeH6: pressure-reservoir gate
Xu et al., npj Computational Materials12,183, DOI10.1038/s41524-026-02040-x. Publisher displays published28March2026 and version-of-record21May2026; both dates recorded, not silently conflated. Theory: Tc~130K, lambda~2.1; harmonic/SSCHA/AIMD local stability reported but energy above competing phases~0.11eV/atom. This is an unmade theoretical material, not measured superconductivity. MgNaFeH6 is dynamically unstable; increasing hole doping further also triggers instability. Merely doping more is not a supported upward path.

Published decomposition:6MgAlFeH6→5AlH3+3Mg2FeH6+AlFe3H+H2. `phase_access_review.py` balances it with pymatgen (54atoms on each side), uses reported~0.11eV/atom directly, and obtains approximately−5.94eV per reaction. Only oneH2 is released, so the reservoir-only compensation would need~+5.94eV/H2 relative to that static reference. A differential six-decade fugacity increase contributes only6.61meV/atom at300K or22.05meV/atom at1000K, holding all solid terms fixed. This is NOT an absolute pressure prediction: static H2 energy is not a1bar finite-T gas reference, solid PΔV/structural changes/ZPE/free energies are absent, and high-pressure synthesis is not excluded. It shows why a full balanced pressure-dependent phase diagram matters rather than a qualitative 'increase hydrogen pressure' argument. No candidate:130K below bar and no demonstrated uplift route.
